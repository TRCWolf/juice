### How would you pitch your game in one line?

A roguelike with no in combat luck, as well no sense of tone, with everything in the game being tonally different.
---

### What is the key mechanic(s) that make it fun?

**1. Little to no in combat RNG**. A lot of roguelikes will have you lose because you got an unlucky situation in combat, but in this game you will only lose because of mistakes or poor planning on the map.
**2. Slay the spire inspired mapping:** Slay the spire had a really good mapping system. In this game, it will have a similary complex mapping system based off of time, where you can only visit so many destionations before you have to face a boss.

**3. Interesting tone:** The game is planned to have no tonal continuity. Some enemies will be serious, some will be silly, some will be dark. The idea is to create an abusrd world where you have no clue what you will find next.

---

### How does the game actually play? (Core Game Loop)

1. **Start at the Trailhead:** Players embark on a path representing their kingdom's journey, with levels unlocked by completing mini-games.

2. **Mini-Games:**
   - **Zen Garden:** Players arrange objects (stones, plants, water) to restore harmony and earn coins for successful placements.
   - **Tower Strategist:** Players defend their kingdom by placing towers to stop waves of enemies trying to steal resources.
   - **Treasure Hunt:** Players explore a grid to uncover treasures and avoid traps, earning prosperity points for their kingdom.

3. **Build and Upgrade Village:** Coins earned from mini-games are used to develop and protect the kingdom’s resources.

4. **Repeat and Explore:** Players choose between following the trail’s main progression or replaying their favorite games for extra rewards.

---

### How will you keep players engaged for 30 minutes? What's the general scope of your game (will change as you go)?

1. **Mapping** Time starts at 0. When you do something on the map, it increases by 1. The map will have many destionations, but only so many to visit. Some destinations will be locked behind completing others. The goal of the map is to make it a risk/reward management system, where risky locations give you more loot."

2. **Combat** Most of the map spaces will have a combat. This is a JRPG combat will 4 party members.

3. **Boss:** When time = 6, you have to fight a powerful boss. Then, after winning, time resets back to zero.


**Scope:** The initial game will feature:
- 5 bosses main bosses that you have to defeat. After beating the 5th boss, you go to a final boss area which has not been designed yet.
- A RPG combat system with many different strategies, but clearly defined party roles. Each character does 1 thing, but there are many ways to go about doing that one thing.
- A mapping system with many different map events from enemies, bosses, mystery events, shop, treasure and more. All built around risk vs reward.
